What eval3r covers

Prediction I/O

Write point clouds, meshes, trajectories, camera intrinsics, metadata, and a manifest that records explicit assumptions.

Metrics

Compute geometry metrics with explicit Chamfer variants, thresholds, alignment choices, sample counts, and seeds.

Benchmarks

Run a method across ScanNet, Replica, DTU, ETH3D, or Tanks & Temples splits, discover predictions by pattern, and produce reproducible JSON and CSV summaries.
