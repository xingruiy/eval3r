Prediction Format

A prediction directory is a small filesystem contract centered on eval3r_prediction.json.

Directory Layout

scene0799_00/
  eval3r_prediction.json
  geometry/
    pred_points.ply
    pred_mesh.ply
  trajectory/
    pred_trajectory_tum.txt
    pred_trajectory_kitti.txt
  cameras/
    intrinsics.json
  aux/
    custom_arrays.npy

Manifest Fields

Field Purpose
scene_id, dataset, method Identify the prediction and the method that produced it.
unit One of m, cm, mm, or unspecified.
coordinate_system One of colmap, opengl, opencv, or unspecified.
pose_convention One of T_wc, T_cw, or unspecified.
geometry, trajectory, cameras Artifact paths, formats, hashes, counts, and optional extra metadata.

Artifact Integrity

Files referenced from the manifest include relative paths and SHA-256 hashes. This makes it possible to detect stale, missing, or modified prediction artifacts during validation.
